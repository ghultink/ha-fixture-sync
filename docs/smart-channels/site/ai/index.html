Why not feed the AI the whole catalogue?

On-device AI models have small context windows. Real publications have thousands of clips. So the AI is used as a topic classifier only — given a short theme and the buckets the site organises around, it picks 1–3 buckets. The Channel's existing smart-cliplist machinery then does the actual search.
