2026 F1 cars revealed — no DRS, Marko hints at internal Red Bull truce

By GP Daily Sports Desk · June 11, 2024

The FIA has unveiled the first images of the 2026 F1 car, alongside detailed engine-regulation updates. Helmut Marko has separately commented on what he called a 'ceasefire' inside Red Bull Racing.

The 2026 regulations end DRS in its current form. The chassis becomes ~30kg lighter and the powertrain split moves to a 50/50 ICE/electric balance. The end-screen panel of the video above will refresh from the smart playlist whenever you change the topic — try it in the side panel.

Why the related items panel changes: the embedded player loads a smart playlist that filters videos by cat. The filter value isn't hard-coded — it's read from the player URL's ?topic=… at runtime. Different articles → different topic → different recommendations, from one playlist and one playout config.
What's happening under the hood?
  1. The player loads the playout config. It sees relatedItems: "Show" and exitscreenItemsListId: 1778574689889505.
  2. It fetches the cliplist. Cliplist has allowDatasource: true so the player doesn't trust the static filter value.
  3. DataSourceHelper resolves each filter's datasource. Our filter says sourceType=QueryString, sourceValue=topic — so the player reads its own URL's ?topic=….
  4. It POSTs the resolved filterset back to /json/search and renders the items on the post-roll end screen.
