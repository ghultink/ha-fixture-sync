Show the right videos at the end of every clip — automatically

After a video ends, our player shows a panel of follow-up videos. The smart thing? What appears in that panel can adapt to the article around it — without anyone reconfiguring the player per page.

Why this matters

Problem. Most players just show "more from the same channel" at the end. If you embed the same video on a recipe article and on a parenting article, both visitors see the same generic list.

What the website already knows. The site you're reading already knows what each article is about — that's why the same article appears under "Recipes", "Real estate", "Parenting" or "Gaming" in the menu. That knowledge is sitting right there in the URL, in the article tags, or in the visitor's profile.

The smart bit. We let the website hand that knowledge to the player — and the panel updates itself. A recipe article ends in more recipes. A gaming article ends in more gaming. The visitor stays longer, watches more.

Six ways the page can guide what plays

Five techniques drive the end-screen strip after a fixed anchor clip; the sixth lets the player pick the main clip itself. Same publication, same video pool — different points in the funnel.

1

The URL

The article's web address says what topic it's about. The player reads that and matches.

SIMPLEST · No code changes

 2

The visitor's preference

The visitor picks what they like; the choice is remembered in their browser and follows them around the site.

PERSONAL · Sticks per visitor

 3

The article's hidden tag

Articles already carry hidden labels for SEO ("this is a parenting article"). The player reads the same labels.

INVISIBLE · Editors keep working their way

 4

A ready-made list

If the site already has its own recommendation engine, it hands the player a finished list of clips.

FULL CONTROL · Bypass the engine

 5

The visitor's own AI

The visitor's browser-based AI (Gemini Nano, ChatGPT, …) picks the best follow-ups from the catalogue.

ON-DEVICE · No keys to manage

 6

The article's own SEO PICKS THE MAIN CLIP

Unlike the other five — which all leave the main clip fixed and only swap the strip after — Suggests reads the article's existing SEO metadata and lets the Blue Billywig search engine pick which clip plays. The end-screen strip is filled from the rest of that same search.

BUILT-IN · No extra signal required

How to use this demo

  1. Pick one of the six scenarios above.
  2. In the side panel, choose a topic (Recipes, Real estate, Parenting, Gaming).
  3. Hit Reload, then click Skip to end on the player.
  4. Watch the bottom strip — those follow-ups were chosen by the smart playlist (or, in scenario ⑤, by the AI + a SAPI search) using whichever signal that page demonstrates.
