In plain English: Every article on a modern website already carries hidden labels — the things Google and Facebook read to know what an article is about. We let the player read those same labels. Editors keep tagging articles the way they always have, and the right follow-up videos appear at the end. Nothing changes in the editor's workflow; nothing changes in the URL; the visitor doesn't have to click anything.
